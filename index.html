Clear Specifications & What to Expect

Select any care category below to see detailed scope, who it helps, included support, and safety boundaries.

👵 Elderly Home Care

What it is: Holistic daily living assistance designed for seniors who wish to age gracefully in their own homes.

What's Included:

  • ✓ Bathing, grooming & dressing assistance
  • ✓ Meal planning, preparation & feeding support
  • ✓ Medication reminders & hydration tracking
  • ✓ Mobility, walking & transfer support
  • ✓ Light housekeeping & laundry assistance
  • ✓ Companionship, conversation & mental stimulation
  • ✓ Errand assistance & medical appointment support

What Isn't Included:

  • ✕ Medical procedures (e.g. IV administration, surgical wound dressing)
  • ✕ Prescribing or changing medical dosages
  • ✕ Heavy home construction or deep masonry repairs
  • ✕ Emergency medical transportation
Medical Notice: Caregivers do not perform medical procedures unless appropriately licensed, certified, and authorized under explicit clinical supervision.

🏠 Personal Home Care

Personal hygiene, routine daily upkeep, and respectful assistance tailored to client comfort.

What's Included:

  • ✓ Personal hygiene & bath safety supervision
  • ✓ Dressing & wardrobe coordination
  • ✓ Mealtime assistance & dietary tracking
  • ✓ Bed linen changes & light room cleaning

Safety Boundaries:

Caregivers provide non-clinical support. Any clinical nursing needs can be arranged through authorized healthcare partners.

🏥 Post-Hospital Care

Dedicated home support following surgery, discharge, or illness to reduce re-hospitalization risks.

What's Included:

  • ✓ Discharge care plan execution support
  • ✓ Fall prevention & movement guidance
  • ✓ Prescription pickup & routine reminders
  • ✓ Follow-up appointment accompaniment

Clinical Disclaimer:

Does not include invasive clinical care unless specifically arranged with licensed registered nurses.

Safety & Verification

Our 5-Tier Caregiver Screening Guarantee

Your safety, privacy, and dignity are our highest priorities. We enforce strict screening before any caregiver enters a home.

1. Identity Verification

Government ID checks, official biometric verification, and legal right-to-work audits.

2. Background Checks

Comprehensive criminal history checks where legally available and clean record verification.

3. Reference Verification

Rigorous direct interviews with past healthcare supervisors, clients, and families.

4. Practical Evaluation

Hands-on practical tests for mobility transfer, hygiene care, and emergency CPR protocols.

5. Ongoing Supervision

Regular unannounced quality checks and ongoing care coordinator oversight.

Medical Emergency Guidance

Important Safety Notice

HavenCare Home Services is a non-emergency personal home care service. We do not provide emergency medical procedures or immediate ambulance dispatch.

If you or your loved one is experiencing chest pain, difficulty breathing, severe bleeding, stroke symptoms, or sudden loss of consciousness:

  • 🚨 Call National Emergency Services: 999 / 911 / 112
  • 🚑 Contact Nearest Hospital Emergency Room
